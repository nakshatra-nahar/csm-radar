# Model card: tallyworks

Generated by `radar eval --tenant tallyworks` from the synthetic world. Each CSM works up to 10 accounts from their book each week.

## Summary

- In an average test week the model's top 10 per CSM held 43% (95% CI 35% to 51%) of the ARR that went on to churn within 90 days.
- Sorting by renewal date held 18%, the rules heuristic 47% and random picks 20%.
- Renewal sort does worse than random because accounts near renewal have mostly already announced their decision and left the worklist, so the ones left are mostly renewers.
- Model minus renewal sort: +24 points (95% CI +14 to +35). Model minus rules: -4 points (95% CI -11 to +3).
- An oracle that sees true account health reaches 67%: the gap is what better data could still buy.
- All acceptance gates pass.

## How it was tested

Snapshots are Mondays. 4 origins, 8 weeks apart, cover the last 32 weeks with settled labels (2025-11-03 to 2026-06-08). At each origin the model is refit on snapshots at least 97 days older, labelled only with what had arrived by the origin, then scored on the next 8 weeks. Test labels use full hindsight. A book is the accounts a CSM owns at the snapshot.

## Recall@K by policy

Recall (ARR-weighted) and precision are computed each week across every CSM's book, then averaged over test weeks. Lift is precision over the base rate. Every policy ranks the same rows. As served, a slot takes only an account at or above the slot floor (lift at least min_slot_lift; for the rules heuristic, rules_score at the yellow cut), so a book can get fewer than K; random and the sorts have no floor.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Random (K/N per book) | 20% (95% CI 19% to 20%) | 7% | 1.0x | 0.50 | 0.07 |  |

## Ranking quality and calibration

Reliability of the model's probability, 10 quantile bins per segment. Design §6 adds a Platt step only if the error (ECE) exceeds 0.02 in a segment with at least 300 rows.

- billed monthly: 13,351 rows, ECE 0.017
- billed yearly: 6,146 rows, ECE 0.019

## Lead time

Of 174 churn decisions in the test weeks, the model flagged 90 (in a top 10 for 2 weeks in a row) before the decision. Median lead: 10.8 weeks. 34% of all decisions were flagged at least 6 weeks ahead. The label horizon caps lead time at about 13 weeks.

## Confidence intervals

Paired cluster bootstrap over accounts, 1,000 resamples: an account's weeks stay together, and both policies are scored on the same resample.

- Model minus renewal sort: +24 points (95% CI +14 to +35).
- Model minus rules: -4 points (95% CI -11 to +3).

## Leakage check: hindsight vs point-in-time

| features | recall@K | ROC-AUC |
|---|---|---|
| point-in-time (deployed) | 43% | 0.671 |
| hindsight: late data arrives on time | | 0.672 |
| naive current-state: today's Salesforce row | 97% | 0.969 |

Hindsight features use every record as if it had arrived when it happened: +0.001 AUC, so late arrivals barely change this world's weekly snapshots.
The naive current-state model sees each Salesforce account row (Type, ARR__c, Renewal_Date__c) as it stands at history end in every snapshot. The registry computes the same features from it, plus former_customer, so a churned account is already a Former Customer; its +0.298 AUC is the optimism the as-of rule prevents.

## Acceptance gates

- Oracle ROC-AUC 0.843 (band 0.80 to 0.92).
- Best single feature: seat_utilization, ROC-AUC 0.695 (at most 0.72).
- Full observable model, in sample: ROC-AUC 0.715 (at most 0.86).
- Model recall@K beats renewal sort: yes.
- Coefficient signs: all match.

Result: all gates pass.

## Known limits

- Seat changes carry weak signal: seat_change_180d alone has ROC-AUC 0.53. usage_drop also moves when a customer cuts seats, so some seat-cut risk shows up as a usage drop.
- Capacity is a cap: a slot never goes to an account below the base rate. A large account with a low p drops off the list even when its expected ARR at risk would rank it in, and a healthy book gets fewer than K.
- Gradient boosting is a diagnostic row: does the linear model miss structure? It has no serving path.
- Causes with few decisions have noisy recall. The world is synthetic: these numbers show the method works, not what a real book will do.
