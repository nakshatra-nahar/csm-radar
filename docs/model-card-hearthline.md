# Model card: hearthline

Generated by `radar eval --tenant hearthline` from the synthetic world. Each CSM works the top 10 accounts in their book each week.

## Summary

- Each week the model's top 10 per CSM held 62% (95% CI 54% to 69%) of the ARR that went on to churn within 90 days.
- Sorting by renewal date held 14%, the rules heuristic 54% and random picks 26%.
- Renewal sort does worse than random because accounts near renewal have mostly already announced their decision and left the worklist, so the ones left are mostly renewers.
- Model minus renewal sort: +48 points (95% CI +39 to +56). Model minus rules: +8 points (95% CI +3 to +13).
- An oracle that sees true account health reaches 79%: the gap is what better data could still buy.
- All acceptance gates pass.

## How it was tested

Snapshots are Mondays. 4 origins, 8 weeks apart, cover the last 32 weeks with settled labels (2025-11-03 to 2026-06-08). At each origin the model is refit on snapshots at least 97 days older, labelled only with what had arrived by the origin, then scored on the next 8 weeks. Test labels use full hindsight. A book is the accounts a CSM owns at the snapshot.

## Ranking quality and calibration

Reliability of the model's probability, 10 quantile bins per segment. Design §6 adds a Platt step only if the error (ECE) exceeds 0.02 in a segment with at least 300 rows.

- billed monthly: 7,387 rows, ECE 0.014
- billed yearly: 12,623 rows, ECE 0.024 (needs Platt)

## Lead time

Of 190 churn decisions in the test weeks, the model flagged 127 (in a top 10 for 2 weeks in a row) before the decision. Median lead: 12.3 weeks. 58% of all decisions were flagged at least 6 weeks ahead. The label horizon caps lead time at about 13 weeks.

## Confidence intervals

Paired cluster bootstrap over accounts, 1,000 resamples: an account's weeks stay together, and both policies are scored on the same resample.

- Model minus renewal sort: +48 points (95% CI +39 to +56).
- Model minus rules: +8 points (95% CI +3 to +13).

## Leakage check: hindsight vs point-in-time

Model ROC-AUC with features as they were known at each snapshot: 0.768. With hindsight features that ignore when data arrived: 0.769. The difference (+0.001) is the optimism a pipeline without the as-of rule would report.

## Acceptance gates

- Oracle ROC-AUC 0.848 (band 0.80 to 0.92).
- Best single feature: seat_utilization, ROC-AUC 0.717 (at most 0.72).
- Full observable model, in sample: ROC-AUC 0.753 (at most 0.86).
- Model recall@K beats renewal sort: yes.
- Coefficient signs: all match.

Result: all gates pass.

## Known limits

- Seat changes carry weak signal: seat_change_180d alone has ROC-AUC 0.55. usage_drop also moves when a customer cuts seats, so some seat-cut risk shows up as a usage drop.
- Gradient boosting is a diagnostic row: does the linear model miss structure? It has no serving path.
- Causes with few decisions have noisy recall. The world is synthetic: these numbers show the method works, not what a real book will do.
